04 / CUSTOM DROPDOWNS

Choose your next adventure

Custom menus with native radio buttons and checkboxes. Gold highlights, contained menus.

Single selection .xiv-dropdown

City-state:Gridania
Choose one city-state

Select one option to close the menu. Arrow keys navigate native radio options.

Multiple selections .xiv-dropdown--multiple

Duties:Leveling, Trials
Select any number of duties

Keep selecting while the menu stays open. Tab moves between checkboxes; Space toggles them.

Include xiv.js for selected-value summaries, Escape to close, and closing when focus or a click moves outside. Menus expand within their area and scroll for longer lists.

09 / THE GAME PALETTE

The colors of Eorzea

All 125 named dyes from the game’s Stain sheet, using the exact stored RGB values.

Download colors ↓

HUD resource colors Visual reference matches

EXP uses gold, HP green, MP pink. The blue segment shows the rested EXP bonus. These gradient samples are visual approximations of the HUD; the dye palette below preserves exact Stain.Color values.

Experience + rested bonus
.xiv-exp-track + .xiv-exp-earned + .xiv-exp-rested

Source: FFXIV Stain sheet via XIVAPI · Retrieved 12 September 2026. Colors are decoded directly from the 24-bit Stain.Color value as #RRGGBB. All 125 named dyes are included; “No Color” and three unnamed rows are excluded. Metallic dyes show their stored flat swatch color; material shine is not represented by a hex value.
